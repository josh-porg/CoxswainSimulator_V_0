Agreement across raters (Krippendorff's alpha)

Computed on calls and clips with at least two raters, check items and repeats left out. Target uses each rater's main target. Alpha near 1 means raters agree; near 0, no better than chance; around 0.67 is a common lowest acceptable value for tentative conclusions.
