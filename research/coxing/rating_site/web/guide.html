How to rate

Each day: up to 15 calls (a few questions each), then 10 muffled voices. About five minutes. There are no right answers; the study needs your own reading. Please don't discuss particular calls with other raters.

Use the same username every time, on every device, so your ratings stay together. If the page asks for your details again on a new phone, give the same username.

The calls

The highlighted line is the call. The grey lines before and after are what was said around it, to help you read it. The words come from automatic transcription in a moving boat, so some are misheard; names are replaced by [name].

Count or other on its own skips the remaining questions.

What does it do?

states a fact ("you're at a 30"), judges how it's going ("that's better", "too high"), or tells the crew what to do ("sit up").

Does it name a concrete action?

Yes if it names a movement, force or rhythm change the crew can do ("hands away quickly", "slower up the slide"). No if it only judges, informs or encourages ("cleaner at the finish" is borderline: it names where, not what).

Does it raise or lower intensity?

Raises: urges intensity or urgency ("now", "go", "attack", "give me everything"). Lowers: calms or settles ("relax", "settle", "breathe"). Otherwise neither.

Not sure, or misheard?

Give your best answer and tap not sure or words look misheard. Don't spend long on any one call.

The voices

Each clip is a call with the words filtered out, so only the loudness, pitch and pace come through. Play it to the end (the buttons unlock then), and rate how fired-up the voice sounds, from 1 (very calm) to 7 (very fired-up). Rate the voice, not what you guess was said.

Why a daily limit?

Short daily sessions keep ratings fresh and spread them over different days. Come back tomorrow for new ones. A few calls and clips come round again on purpose, to check how steady ratings are.

Your data

Your answers and profile are kept with your username and never shown to other raters. The calls and clips on this site are encrypted and can only be opened with the full link you were sent; please don't pass it on. You can stop at any time, and the study lead can delete your answers if you ask.
