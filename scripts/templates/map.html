The dashed line is the depth at which this boat reaches Frh = 1. Water shallower than that puts the hull on the far side of the transcritical drag rise, where a watt buys almost no speed.

Fields are the surveyed bathymetry and the modelled sheltered wind, not live data. The water-level slider shifts every depth by a constant, which is what a dry or wet year does to first order and is not a re-survey.
