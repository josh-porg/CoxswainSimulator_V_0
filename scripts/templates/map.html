Which side to make them yield

The rulebook gives the passer the choice of side and says nothing about which is better. The river does: bars above the axis mean port is the worse water for them, so that is the side to name. Height is the speed they lose, in percent.

Depth along the centreline

Dashed line is the depth at which this boat reaches Frh = 1. Shallower than that puts the hull on the far side of the transcritical drag rise.

Surveyed bathymetry and the modelled sheltered wind, not live data. The water-level slider shifts every depth by a constant, which is what a dry or wet year does to first order and is not a re-survey.
